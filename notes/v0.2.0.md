## See the road

Course rides now show the road ahead, right in the terminal: an
animated view drawn from your GPX and from OpenStreetMap.

- **Bends and hills from your route.** The road curves where your GPX
  curves and climbs where it climbs; a crest hides what lies behind it,
  and the view tilts with the grade.
- **The land along it.** Fields with rows of crops, forests, meadows,
  orchards, heath, water, and the houses, farms and sheds beside the
  road, all from OpenStreetMap.
- **Your ghost on the road.** When you race your best time, your ghost
  rides ahead of you, see-through, as a magenta rider.
- **Smooth at 20 frames a second**, between the core's updates.
- Power, grade, time and distance to go sit above the road. `v` swaps
  the road for the big numbers of 0.1.0 and back.

## Race any ride, not just your best

In HISTORY, press `enter` on any finished course ride to ride that
course again with that ride as your ghost, from where it started. In
ACTIVITIES, `enter` on a recorded course ride does the same (`s` still
saves the file).

## Keys

- `r` is for rides: COURSES, HISTORY and ACTIVITIES.
- `w` is for workouts: your workouts and the editor, with Fixed power
  (ERG) at the top for a ride at one power.
- `?`, `h` or F1 shows every key for the screen you're on.

## Your numbers, your way

- Course rides and workouts show your cadence, averaged over 5 seconds;
  in a workout, with the cadence the workout asks for.
- `o` arranges the tiles of the dashboard, a course ride or a workout:
  pick from more numbers (heart rate, speed, average power, climbed,
  time left) and put them in your order.
- A second, medium digit size fits more tiles; the screen picks the
  largest that fits, and `z` keeps it medium. Kept in
  `~/osscycler/tui.json` on the machine running the screen.

## Map data, and your privacy

To draw the land, the core downloads OpenStreetMap data around each of
your courses from the public Overpass server (overpass-api.de): once per
course, in the background, kept in `~/osscycler/courses/.osm/`. It
downloads again only when you change a GPX file. Until the data is
there, or if the server is busy, the road runs through grass and trees;
the core tries again after 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes.

The server gets the area around each route, and nothing else (no
account, no name). Routes you recorded yourself often start at home,
so that area includes your home. **This is on by default.** To keep your
routes to yourself, start the core with `-osm=false`; the road view still
works, without the scenery. See "Map data" in the README.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open
Database License; the road view shows this credit.

## Good to know

- The road view needs both programs from this release: an 0.1.0 core
  doesn't send the route's shape, and the 0.2.0 screen then shows the
  0.1.0 ride screen.
- Only a ride that crosses the finish line counts for your best time and
  your ghost. An aborted ride is still recorded as a FIT file.
- Tried so far: the road view on recorded routes with a simulated rider.
