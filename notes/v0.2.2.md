## The license comes with the download

0.2.1 was the first release under the GNU General Public License
version 3, but its downloads didn't include the license text. Now every
archive has a LICENSE file next to the programs, and the packages
install it where Debian and Fedora keep a package's license
(`/usr/share/doc/osscycler/copyright` and
`/usr/share/licenses/osscycler/LICENSE`).

## Good to know

- The programs are the same as in 0.2.0 and 0.2.1: no need to update
  for anything new to ride.
