## A route to ride, right away

osscycler now comes with a course: the **Posbank Loop**, 32 km over the
Veluwezoom in the Netherlands. It starts on the main road through
Rheden, climbs the Diepesteeg to the Posbank, crosses the Veluwe to
Eerbeek, and comes back down through Dieren to Rheden: 163 m of
climbing, up to 7.9 %. Find it in COURSES (`r`), with your own GPX
files.
It is built into the programs, so there is nothing to download or
install.

The route follows OpenStreetMap's roads, with heights from the Dutch
national elevation model (AHN). It stays on the road everywhere: no
detours through car parks, no out-and-back spurs.

## More to see along the road

The road view now shows signs of people in the landscape, sparingly:

- **Junctions** where your route turns onto another road, or crosses a
  main road. The other roads leave the junction as they do on the map.
- **Car parks** beside the road, with a few parked cars.
- **Place-name signs** where you enter a town or village, and the
  place's name under the road for the first 500 m.
- Roadside trees stay in view until you pass them, and the view sits at
  a rider's eye height (1.6 m).
- A road split into two one-way carriageways is drawn as two narrow
  roads, not two full-width ones.

## Map data only for the course you ride

The core used to download map data for every course in your folder.
Now it downloads only for a course you ride, when you start the ride:
5 km at a time, starting where you start, while you are already riding.
Scenery that arrives during your ride appears on the road as it comes,
without restarting the screen. The data is kept in
`~/osscycler/courses/.osm/`, and smaller than before (the Posbank Loop
needs about 11 MB). It is downloaded again only if you change a GPX
file.

To get the data, the core still sends the area around the course to the
public Overpass server, and nothing else. Routes you recorded yourself
often start at home, so the area can include your home. To keep your
routes to yourself, start the core with `-osm=false`; the road view
still works, without scenery. See "Map data" in the README.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open
Database License. The credit is now in the start menu, and on the ride
screen for the first 20 seconds of every ride, instead of using up part
of the ride screen for the whole ride.

## The license comes with the download

Every archive now has a LICENSE file next to the programs, and the
packages install it where Debian and Fedora keep a package's license
(`/usr/share/doc/osscycler/copyright` and
`/usr/share/licenses/osscycler/LICENSE`). 0.2.1 shipped without it.

## Good to know

- Update both programs. A 0.2 screen with this core works, but it
  doesn't pick up scenery that arrives during a ride.
- Recordings import into Garmin Connect: a free ride shows as an indoor
  ride, with speed, distance, heart rate, cadence and power.
- Tried so far: the Posbank Loop with a simulated rider, laps of the
  400 m oval and ERG workouts on a Tacx Flux 2. No full ride of the
  Posbank Loop on a real trainer yet.

## For developers

- All Go packages except `gen` (the API's protobuf bindings) now live
  under `internal/`: `gen` is the only one meant to be imported.
- `osscycler-replay` also replays rides on the test tracks and the
  Posbank Loop.
- The simulated rider (`-fake`) now pushes harder uphill as a rider
  does, so the difficulty setting shows in its power.
- Dependabot keeps the Go modules and GitHub Actions up to date.
- `make lint` runs staticcheck under Go 1.27.1 for now: the newest
  staticcheck can't read Go 1.27.2's compiled packages yet.
